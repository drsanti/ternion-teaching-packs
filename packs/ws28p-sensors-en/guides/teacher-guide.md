# Sensors with the ws28p board — teacher guide

Three lessons with the robotos ws28p board (ESP32-S3): its buttons, its motion sensor and reaction time.

## Equipment

- One ws28p board and a USB data cable per pair of students, and a laptop with Chrome or Edge.
- **No boards?** Every lesson also runs on a simulated board: on the "Connect your board" slide, press **No board here? Use a simulated robotos board** under the card (only the presenter sees it). Reaction times are only meaningful with a real board.

## Lessons

1. **Meet the board** (30 min) — What is on a ws28p, connecting it, and its two buttons.
2. **Tilt and gravity** (40 min) — The accelerometer, three axes, and reading tilt from gravity.
3. **Reaction time** (35 min) — Measure how fast you press a button, then compare and improve.

The quizzes and polls need a live class (Present → Start a class). Speaker notes on every slide say what to do and say.
