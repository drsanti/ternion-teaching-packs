# Introduction to TERNION — teacher guide

Three short lessons that teach teachers to make, present and teach with TERNION. No equipment is needed: lesson 2 uses built-in demo signals and lesson 3 a simulated board.

## Before the course

- Add the three lessons from the Teaching Library (each is your own copy; change anything).
- Try presenting each deck once.

## Lessons

1. **Your first deck** (30 min) — The editor, adding content, themes and presenting.
2. **Live data and widgets** (40 min) — Sources, channels, gauges and charts that move with real data. Connect the demo source in Live data, or press L while presenting.
3. **The interactive classroom** (40 min) — Polls, quizzes, board checks and runnable code with a class. Start a class so students can answer on their phones.

Speaker notes on every slide say what to do and say.
