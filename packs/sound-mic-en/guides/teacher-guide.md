# Sound and the microphone — teacher guide

Three lessons on sound, measured live with your laptop's microphone. Only numbers (loudness, pitch, beats) are used; the sound is never recorded or sent anywhere.

## Before the course

- Add the three lessons from the Teaching Library (each is your own copy).
- Open a lesson, press Present, then press **L** to connect the microphone and allow it when the browser asks. Try the live slides once.
- Press Run on one exercise, so the Python runtime is downloaded on your computer.

## Lessons

1. **Loudness** (35 min) — Sound as a vibration, how loud it is, and how fast it travels.
2. **Pitch and frequency** (40 min) — High and low notes, hertz, wavelength and octaves.
3. **Beats and a clap counter** (35 min) — Detecting beats, counting claps from data, and tempo in beats per minute.

Each lesson has two graded Python exercises with a teacher-only solution slide; one check per lesson is hidden and graded on your computer. Quizzes need a live class (Present ▾ → Start a class).

## Tips

- A quiet room makes the live slides clearer; the built-in laptop microphone is enough.
- Pitch reads best from one steady hummed or sung note.
